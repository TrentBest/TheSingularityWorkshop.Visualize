Change the lighting

Try azimuth −90°, 0°, 90°, then 180°. Watch the bright region move around the fixed sphere. Lower ambient light to expose the difference between surfaces facing the light and surfaces turned away.

01 · The normal

A normal is a direction perpendicular to a surface at a point. This experiment samples a unit sphere, so each surface position also supplies its outward normal.

N = (x, y, z), where x² + y² + z² = 1

02 · Diffuse response

The simple Lambert model brightens a surface in proportion to how directly it faces the light. Light from behind the surface contributes no direct diffuse light.

diffuse = max(0, N · L)

03 · What this proves

Controls change SVG marks and their colour values; no image is swapped in. This is a pedagogical 2D projection of a sampled 3D lighting calculation—not a GPU shader or production material system.
