Soldier Field — Event Horizon Scaling Lab

A procedural science-fiction exoskeleton trooper rendered as real 3D geometry and instanced across eight independently updated Event Horizon workloads. This lab deliberately repeats the selected population in every horizon to compare computational cost even where that many full-size soldiers could not physically fit. It is a stress model, not a claim that Renderer policy is already wired in.

One soldier mesh per draw, many instances per horizon. Each horizon has its own instance buffer and refresh cadence. Physical occupancy, eye tracking, focal-beam detail, animation, and production materials remain future work.
